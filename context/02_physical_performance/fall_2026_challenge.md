# Victor Daily Erg — Fall 2026 Edition

The endurance block. Eleven weeks, one competition, two absurd pieces of distance, and a
5k on the last day with a film star's name on it.

This file is the single source of truth for the challenge. The site parses it — the day
table, the routines and the goals all render from the markdown below, so editing this file
changes the app with no deploy. Keep the table shapes intact; the headings are load-bearing.

## 1. The Challenge

- **Start**: 2026-09-20
- **End**: 2026-12-04
- **Days**: 76
- **Average target**: 5000 m per day
- **Planned total**: 725000 m
- **Required total**: 380000 m

### Rules

- **Rule 1**: at least one erg, perg or boat practice every single day.
- **Rule 2**: average 5000 m per day **across the whole challenge**, not per day. A short day
  is not a failed day; the average is the ledger.
- **Rule 3**: erg and perg are interchangeable. Substitute the machine freely.
- **Rule 4**: boat practice counts as 5000 m toward the average.
- **Rule 5**: runs to the Friday before finals week — 2026-12-04.

### The margin

The plan lays down **725,000 m** against a requirement of **380,000 m**. That is a surplus of
**345,000 m**, or roughly **69 fully missed days** of cushion. Rule 2 will not be what stops
this. Consistency, the lower back, and the two big pieces will be.

### The week's shape

Built around the term: Monday and Wednesday are the lighter academic days and carry the longer
weekday pieces; Tuesday, Thursday and Friday are the heavy academic days and stay at 30 minutes.

| Day | Default |
| --- | ------- |
| Mon | Longest weekday piece, Z2, 45–60 min |
| Tue | 5k or under, not for time, then Strength A |
| Wed | Z2 plus technique, 45–60 min, perg where available |
| Thu | The week's quality session, 30–35 min |
| Fri | Short and easy, then Strength B |
| Sat | Boat practice (coach's session), plus a short shakeout |
| Sun | Boat practice (coach's session), then the week's long piece |

**Polarisation is 85/15.** Thursday carries almost all of the intensity. If a session feels
hard on a day not marked `quality`, `test` or `race`, it is being done wrong.

## 6. Stretching Routines

Fifteen routines, ten minutes each, except the recovery pool at fifteen. The routine is chosen
by the day's `Type` — see §5 — and is fixed for that day, so changing one day's session changes
only that day's routine and never any other.

**Every movement needs nothing but a floor.** No bands, no foam roller, no pull-up bar, no rack,
no step, no bench, no doorway — a dorm room, a hotel room, a corner of the boathouse or a patch
of grass all work. That rules out a few obvious choices on purpose: the couch stretch becomes a
half-kneeling hip flexor stretch, wall slides become floor slides, band pull-aparts become prone
Y-T-Ws, and the dead hang becomes a standing overhead reach.

**The lower-back rehab protocol lives here now**, with one substitution. The couch stretch,
hamstring flossing and glute bridges are distributed across the pools unchanged, so at least one
lands nearly every day. **Pallof presses are out** — they need a band or a cable and there is no
equipment-free version of a loaded anti-rotation press. Dead bugs, bird dogs and side planks
carry that job instead: same target, same "stiffen the trunk before it gets twisted" intent,
nothing to hold. `benchmarks_and_logs.md` §3 remains the reference for why each one exists.

**Pool sizes are not arbitrary.** Each one is at least as large as the most days of that pool
any single week contains, which is what guarantees no routine repeats inside a week: recovery
peaks at four (the Thanksgiving week), base and quality at three, strength and long at two, and
there is never more than one practice day that is not also a long day.

| Pool | Used on | Routines | Busiest week needs | Minutes |
| ---- | ------- | -------: | -----------------: | ------: |
| recovery | recovery, taper days | 4 | 4 | 15 |
| base | base days | 3 | 3 | 10 |
| quality | quality, test, race days | 3 | 3 | 10 |

- **Pool**: recovery

| -------- | ------------ | --- |

## 7. The Two Big Pieces

Both are one sitting. Breaks are allowed; leaving is not. Both replace boat practice that day.

### 50k perg — 2026-10-18

- **Expected**: 7h30m moving, closer to 8h30m elapsed.
- **Split discipline**: whatever the 25k on day 21 felt like, **add 10 seconds per 500 m**. It
  will feel insultingly slow for the first two hours. That is the point.
- **Structure**: 10 × 5k. Three minutes off between blocks, ten minutes off after 25k.
- **Fuelling**: 60–90 g of carbohydrate per hour from the first hour, not the third. Sports
  drink between blocks plus something solid at 15k and 35k. 500–750 ml of fluid per hour with
  sodium in it.
- **Hands**: tape before the start, not after the first hot spot.
- **Abort rule**: any sharp lower-back pain, any numbness, or a split drifting more than 15 s
  per 500 m off target for two consecutive blocks. Stopping at 35k is a good day. An injury in
  October costs the Cup.

### 100k erg — 2026-11-22

- **Expected**: 8h moving at 2:20–2:30, closer to 9h30m elapsed.
- **Split discipline**: set the target split before starting and do not beat it. The first 30k
  must feel like a warmup.
- **Structure**: 10 × 10k. Five minutes off between blocks, fifteen minutes off at 50k.
- **Fuelling**: as above, and start eating in the first 30 minutes. Over eight hours the deficit
  is the enemy, not the fatigue. Roughly 2,500–3,500 kcal will be burned.
- **Seat**: a pad from the start. Stand and walk for one minute every 10k block.
- **Abort rule**: the same three. Stopping at 75k is still the longest thing you have ever done.
