# Cover Letter Template

Read by the cover letter generator on `/private/work/tailor`. Editing this file changes the
shape of every letter it drafts from here on — the model fills these sections in, in order,
using only material from the vault's bullet library. It never invents a section, and it
never invents a fact within one.

## Greeting

Address the hiring manager by name if the posting names one; otherwise address the team or
the role ("Hiring Team", "the \[Company\] Engineering team"). Never "To Whom It May Concern".

## Hook

One to two sentences. Name the role and the company, and the single most relevant thing
about Victor for *this* posting — not a summary of his whole background. This is the only
paragraph that may reference something specific to the company or the posting's own
language, since it is framing rather than a claim about Victor.

## Body

One to two paragraphs. Each claim traces to a specific bullet from the library — a project,
a role, a lab. Lead with whatever the posting actually asks for, not a fixed order. Do not
restate the resume; explain the *why* behind one or two entries the resume only lists.

## Closing

Two to three sentences. Restate interest in the specific role, note availability/timeline if
relevant, and thank them for their consideration. No generic "I look forward to hearing from
you" filler beyond what actually closes the letter — keep it short.

## Notes

Tone: direct, specific, no hedging language ("I believe I would be a great fit"). Let the
material carry the claim. Victor edits every draft before sending; nothing here is meant to
go out unread.
