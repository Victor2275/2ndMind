# Research Paper — RL for Autonomous Navigation

A first-author paper on reinforcement-learning navigation for autonomous ground vehicles,
written alongside ongoing work at Dimaag.ai. In progress as of 2026-08-29.

The method sits between a learned policy and an engineered controller rather than replacing
one with the other, and the paper's subject is what that buys: motion that stays inside the
vehicle's physical limits, and constraints that hold during training instead of being
penalised after the fact.
